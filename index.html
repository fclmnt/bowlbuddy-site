Questions, answered.

What is BowlBuddy?

BowlBuddy is a bowling score keeper and stats tracker for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, available on the App Store. You tap the pins left standing after each ball, and it keeps the score like a center scoreboard, then shows your average, strike and spare percentages, pin leaves and league average.

Is BowlBuddy free?

Yes. Scoring, sessions, leagues, core stats, iCloud sync and CSV export are free, with unlimited games. BowlBuddy Pro, a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase shared with Family Sharing, adds pin leaves and conversion rates, trends, league handicap, the Apple Watch app, the arsenal, a PDF season report, widgets and Siri.

How do I keep a bowling score on my iPhone?

In BowlBuddy, start a game and tap the pins still standing after each ball, then press Enter. A strike or a spare takes two taps. The app adds strike and spare bonuses, marks splits, and follows the tenth-frame rules. You can also enter pin counts ball by ball, or only the final score.

How is a bowling score calculated?

A game has ten frames. A frame scores the pins knocked down with its two balls. A strike (all ten pins on the first ball) scores 10 plus the next two balls. A spare (all ten pins with two balls) scores 10 plus the next ball. A strike or spare in the tenth frame earns one or two extra balls. A perfect game, twelve strikes in a row, scores 300. BowlBuddy does this math for you.

What is spare conversion, and how does BowlBuddy track it?

Spare conversion is how often you knock down the pins left after your first ball. Because BowlBuddy records which pins were standing, it lists every leave you get, such as the 10 pin, the 6-10 or the 3-6-10, with how many times you converted it. It also splits your rate into single pins, makeable spares and splits, and computes a what-if average with every single pin made.

How is a league average calculated?

A league average is your total pinfall in the league divided by the number of games, truncated to a whole number rather than rounded, as most leagues do. BowlBuddy keeps it for each league, week by week, and can apply league handicap and no-tap rules with Pro.

Can I keep score on my Apple Watch?

Yes, with BowlBuddy Pro. The Watch app shows the pin deck, so you can score each ball from your wrist, check the frames and see your final score and series. Games sync to your iPhone.

Do I need an account?

No. BowlBuddy uses the Apple Account you are already signed in with. Your scores sync through your private iCloud database, and there is no BowlBuddy server.

Can the developer see my scores?

No. Everything you enter stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

Which devices does BowlBuddy run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26 or later, and Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later. The Watch app is part of Pro. There is no Android version.

What happens to my games if Pro ends?

You keep all of them. You can still view, edit, delete and export everything you recorded; only the Pro features lock.

Can I move my scores from another app?

BowlBuddy imports its own CSV format, which you can fill in from a spreadsheet. Export and import are in Settings.
