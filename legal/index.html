  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. We have no server.

  • When you search for a bowling center, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps. The app never asks for your location.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your name, email address or payment details.
  • This website uses no cookies and no analytics.
  • The only personal information we ever receive is what you choose to send us by email.

Franck Clement, publisher of BowlBuddy, is responsible for this policy and is the person in charge of the protection of personal information. For any privacy question or request, email support@bowlbuddy.download.

This policy follows Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Quebec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector. 2. What the app stores

The app stores what you enter so it can work:

  • bowler profiles: a name, handedness and an optional starting average;
  • sessions, games, frames and each ball, with optional notes, lane, oil pattern, lineup board and target arrow;
  • leagues (name, night, season dates, handicap settings) and bowling centers (name and city);
  • your arsenal: ball names, brands, weights, colors and notes;
  • your preferences, such as the accent color, and whether BowlBuddy Pro was active at the last check, so widgets and the Watch work before the App Store responds;
  • simple counters used to decide when to show Apple’s rating prompt.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else, except as described in sections 3 to 6.

3. iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app keeps your records in the private database of your own iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit, so they sync between your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. This database belongs to your Apple Account: as the developer, we have no access to its contents. Apple stores and protects it under Apple’s Privacy Policy, and it counts toward your iCloud storage.

To keep devices in sync, Apple’s push notification service sends silent notifications to the app when your data changes. These come from Apple’s servers, not ours, and are not shown to you.

If iCloud is off or unavailable, your records stay only on the device. Depending on your settings, iOS may also include the app’s data in your device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup).

4. Bowling center search

When you search for a bowling center, the app sends the text you type to Apple Maps to find matching places. The app does not request or use your location. Apple handles these searches under its own privacy policy and does not link them to your Apple Account. If you add a center by hand, nothing is sent.

5. Apple Watch, widgets and Siri

  • Apple Watch: the iPhone and the Watch exchange your game, each ball you score and your Pro status directly with each other, using Apple’s WatchConnectivity.
  • Widgets and Live Activity: the app saves a small summary (such as your average and the current game) in storage shared with its widgets on the same device, so they can display it.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: if you use them, Apple processes your voice requests under Apple’s privacy policy. The app only receives the result, such as a league and a score.

6. Exports and sharing

CSV exports, score card images and PDF season reports are created on your device. They go only where you choose to send or save them, through the iOS share sheet. Shared score cards carry a “Scored with BowlBuddy” mark. Files you import are read on your device.

7. Notifications

If you try to unlock Pro from the Apple Watch while the iPhone app is in the background, the app may show a local notification on your iPhone to finish the purchase there. It is created on your device. You can turn notifications off at any time in the Settings app.
